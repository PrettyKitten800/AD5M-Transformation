## 3. Assembly Notes for Selected Hardware

1. **Stage 1 (Right Frame Datum Fit):** Print `Y_Rail_Spine_R`, `Rear_Motor_Mount_R`, `Front_BFI_Housing_R`. Check `IMETRX MGN12H 300 mm` Y-rail seat + `11× M3×4.6×5.7` heat-set insert fit (leave the rear-most `Y = +145.5` rail hole empty over the frame PEM notch).
2. **Stage 2 (Pins, Bearings, Shims & Idlers):**
   - **Smooth `F695-2RS` pairs (`6×` bays, `10.2 mm` height):** Stack `2× 0.5 mm` (`5×8×0.5 mm`) shims below + `2×` `F695-2RS` (flanges outward) + `2× 0.5 mm` shims above on `5×20 mm` pins.
   - **FULARR `Ø18 × 8 mm` `20T` toothed idlers (`2×` bays, `9.6 mm` height):** Stack `1× 0.5 mm` shim + `20T` idler + `2× 0.5 mm` shims (`9.5 mm` total) on `5×20 mm` (Right) and `5×30 mm` (Left) pins inside the `Ø18.6 mm` bays.
   - **`uxcell MGN12 300 mm` X-Rail:** Pop off the plastic end limit blocks on both ends before sliding the rail into the `XY_Joint_L/R_Upper` C-channels (`±[134..150]`) and clamping at `±137.5 mm`.
   - **`FULARR 16 × 16 mm` Drive Pulleys:** Mount **hub-down** on the rear motor shafts (`[359..375]` Left, `[370..386]` Right) so the belt groove aligns with `Z_B = 370.0` / `Z_A = 381.0` and clears the top `Motor_DS_Cap` bearings.
3. **Stage 3 (Left Side) & R2 Toolhead:** Print the 7 Left-side parts once Stages 1 & 2 pass fit-check; wait for **R2** before printing the final toolhead chassis (`AJOYIB 24 V 60 W` heater upgrade uses `heater_power: 60` + `MPC_CALIBRATE` in Kalico).
