# EBB42 bridge R5 — rounded corners

Supersedes R4 for printing. Main vertical outline corners have 1.2 mm radii. Exposed edges and transitions have smaller blends, generally 0.5 mm and reduced to 0.3/0.15/0.075 mm where space is limited. Hole rims and the functional screw-bore roof geometry remain sharp. Tiny edges immediately around the board-hole recesses are retained where rounding would disrupt the screw seat or clearance.

No strain-relief mount or clip holes. Board location, 45-degree angle, 31 mm EBB screw pitch and the four original housing screw coordinates are retained. Approximate envelope 60 x 35.5 x 17.85 mm. The broad underside remains flat.

Print EBB42_short_bridge_R5.stl at 100% scale, flat base down, supports OFF. Suggested fit print: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 35–40% infill. Check your layer preview before printing. This is a fit prototype, not a physically tested bracket.

Verification: one valid CAD solid; final STL is one connected, watertight, consistently wound mesh. Four very small export gaps (each below 0.22 mm extent) were closed in the final mesh; the STEP retains the CAD geometry. Horizontal-section checks at 0.2 mm spacing show only small corner departures from a strict 45-degree support envelope (maximum total excess area 0.078 mm2 in a section). No large unsupported ledges were detected. No bracket/housing/board CAD intersections.

Mounting unchanged: four upward-facing cover-hole positions X +/-18 and +/-27 mm, Y -7.498 mm in assembly coordinates, diameter 4.3 mm. Reuse the correct housing screw type with allowance for the 4 mm base. Two M3 EBB holes remain 31 mm apart, 3.3 mm round-bottom bores with pointed roofs. Use washers; verify actual screw/nut stack length and access.

Fit limits unchanged: board reference projects 11.85 mm behind the assumed housing rear plane and rises 19.47 mm above the old cover. Installed motor, stock bearing carriage, printer frame, attached plugs and tube bend are not validated by this CAD. Check full travel by hand before powering.
