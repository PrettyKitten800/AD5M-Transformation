# AD5M inline Eddy toolhead R2 (history, not installed)

Follow-up to the rejected inline Eddy `R1`, designed November 11, 2026. A rigid BambuMod-derived side-interface housing carries a detachable front cradle that holds the Eddy Duo around the outside of the front cooling module, so the stock part-cooling blower and duct stay in place. Read [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the mounting, cooling and clearance notes.

**Status:** CAD only, never printed or fitted. It was designed for the stock feeder/motor, USB Eddy and Bambu X1C hotend, all since replaced (Orbiter v2.5 on the `R8` housing, Eddy on CAN on the side mount `R4`). Keep as design history.

**Not in this repository:** `Toolhead_layout_R2.FCStd` (23 MB). It embeds a local part-cooling duct (`part-fan-short-mk2.stl`) whose upstream source and license are unresolved, so it is kept on the Windows workstation only (`outputs/AD5M_toolhead_R2/` in the local working copy, git-excluded). The STEP/STL exports here are the two proposed printable parts.
