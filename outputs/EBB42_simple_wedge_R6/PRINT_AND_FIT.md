# R6 — simple central wedge

Separate alternative to R5, following the latest approved sketch: ONE rectangular plate plus ONE small central wedge with a triangular side profile. No U opening, separate towers or strain-relief piece.

Base: 60 x 21 x 4 mm. Nominal wedge: 40 mm wide, 8 mm deep, 8 mm high before rounding. Overall printed height approximately 10.77 mm after the rounded wedge tip. Base ends at assembly Y=9 to leave the nominal filament-tube axis at Y=12.12 outside the plate.

Outer edges are rounded: 1 mm on the plate and 0.8 mm on the wedge, with a smaller root blend. Holes are cut afterward and their rims stay sharp.

EBB holes: 31 mm apart; 2.5 mm diameter pilot bores, axes PERPENDICULAR to the 45-degree mounting face. They pass through the wedge and plate, not vertically through the face. M3 screws form/cut their thread directly into the plastic. This is a pilot bore, not a modeled helical thread. The wedge contributes approximately 5.5 mm of material along the screw direction, with additional material in the plate. Aim for about 5 mm screw engagement; do not force a screw into an undersized printed bore or overtighten. Actual grip and stripping strength depend on material and printed bore size and have not been physically tested.

Use separate approximately 4 mm M3 spacers between PCB and wedge for underside component clearance. Select screw length for the actual PCB/spacer/washer stack plus approximately 5 mm plastic engagement. The spacer height is hardware clearance, not an extra modeled structure. Verify against the physical EBB42 V1.2.1 before installation.

Carriage attachment locations unchanged: X=-27,-18,+18,+27 mm, Y=-7.498 mm in the housing reference; four 4.3 mm clearance holes. Reuse the correct housing screw type with allowance for the 4 mm base.

Print the STL at 100% scale, FLAT BASE DOWN, SUPPORTS OFF. Suggested fit print: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 35–40% infill. Rounded bottom edges cause a small outward progression in the first layers; inspect normal first-layer adhesion. The angled 2.5 mm bores bridge a short distance without support.

Checked: valid single CAD solid; STL is one connected, watertight, consistently wound mesh; zero overlap with the supplied BambuMod housing. The simple triangle has no floating ledges. This alternate design changes the board mounting arrangement from R5. A complete PCB/spacer/connector assembly is not included or clearance-certified. Actual extruder/motor, tube routing, stock carriage and frame travel remain physical fit checks. R5 remains available for the user's current test print.
