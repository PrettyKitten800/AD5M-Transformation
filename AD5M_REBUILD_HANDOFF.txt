COMMUNICATIONS AND FLASHING
Planned topology: Pi USB -> Octopus configured as USB-to-CAN bridge -> two-wire CAN -> EBB36. Four-wire toolhead harness carries separate 24 V, ground, CAN H, CAN L. Match bitrates; exactly two 120-ohm termination resistors at physical bus ends. Normally about 60 ohms across H/L with all power off. Termination installation/measurement has not been confirmed.
Octopus V1.1 has a two-pin CAN connector and selectable CAN-120R termination per official image; it is not the older RJ11 arrangement.
Pi initially uses its kit USB-C power adapter. Internal 24 V-to-5 V Pi supply remains a future addition, not a selected installed component.

EBB flashing:
- To power electronics from USB alone, unplug USB, fit VUSB jumper, reconnect a data-capable Pi USB-A -> EBB USB-C cable. 24 V disconnected during this USB-only setup.
- Hold BOOT, press/release RESET, release BOOT for STM32 DFU.
- Run lsusb on Pi; expect 0483:df11 for DFU. No output has been supplied.
- MCU STM32G0B1; reference crystal 8 MHz. Normal CAN communication uses PB0/PB1. Bootloader choice/offset depends on whether flashing Klipper directly or installing Katapult; this workflow has NOT been finalized. Do not invent an existing bootloader state or blindly flash a prebuilt binary.
- Remove VUSB for normal 24 V operation after USB flashing.

Octopus flashing:
- MCU STM32H723. Official Klipper V1.1 sample specifies 25 MHz crystal and 128 KiB bootloader. Verify actual workflow/bootloader before writing; sample is not evidence of completed flashing.
- BOOT0 is a two-pin header, not the yellow RESET button. In BTT's horizontal pinout (power screws left, motors across top), BOOT0 is below the rightmost driver socket and left of the microSD area; brown callout points to its two pins.
- For USB-powered electronics, VBUS is a separate two-pin jumper near the square buzzer, immediately left of four DIP switches in BTT's image. Do not confuse it with BOOT0, fan voltage selectors or CAN termination. It is not needed when normal POWER input supplies the board. Change jumpers with supplies disconnected.
- User was told power off, fit BOOT0, then power for DFU; remove BOOT0 for normal boot after flashing.
- Keep heaters physically disconnected for initial flashing/bring-up. Do not let an old firmware config drive unexpected outputs.
- CAN bridge firmware means Octopus is configured as a CAN node, not necessarily a USB serial device. Actual serials, CAN UUIDs, SSH address, Pi OS, Klipper installation/version, installed bootloaders and firmware status are all unknown.
